Levana Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Levana is a Hebrew calendar and moon app. It has no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not track you across apps or websites. This page explains what the app keeps on your iPhone, what leaves it, and why.

What stays on your iPhone

Levana stores the following on your device only, in the app's own storage:

  • Your settings: holiday calendar (Israel or outside Israel), moon orientation, candle-lighting and Shabbat-end preferences, and your reminder choices.
  • Your location: the city, region, country, time zone and approximate coordinates you chose or that "Use my location" found, so the app can calculate sunset and Shabbat times.
  • Your reflection notes: anything you write in Reflect, with its question and date.

None of this is sent to us. We have no server, and we cannot see your notes, your location or your settings. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Backups. Like most apps, Levana's data is included when your iPhone is backed up, to iCloud Backup if you have it on, or to a computer. Those backups are yours and are governed by Apple's terms; we have no access to them.

Location and city search

Levana only uses location to calculate sunset, candle-lighting and Shabbat times for where you are. You never have to share it: you can type a city name instead.

  • "Use my location" asks iOS for a one-time, approximate position (about 1 km). To find that position, your iPhone may contact Apple's location service, as it does for any app. Levana then sends the coordinate once to Apple's geocoding service to learn the city name, region and time zone. Levana never reads your location in the background.
  • City search sends what you type to Apple Maps to suggest matching places, and sends your choice to Apple Maps to look up its coordinates and time zone.

These requests go to Apple, not to us, and are handled under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive them.

Reminders

Reminders for Rosh Chodesh, the full moon, your monthly reflection, candle lighting and holidays are optional and off until you turn them on. They are scheduled on your iPhone itself; there is no notification server, and nothing about your reminders leaves the device. iOS may show them on your lock screen or on a paired Apple Watch, as with any app's notifications.

Sharing a note

Each reflection note has a Share button. If you use it, the note's text is handed to the iOS share sheet and goes only to the app or person you choose. Levana does not send notes anywhere on its own.

Diagnostics from Apple

If you have opted in to sharing analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide us with anonymized crash reports and aggregated usage statistics for Levana. These come from Apple, contain no notes, locations or names, and cannot be used to identify you. You can turn this off at any time in those settings. Apart from this, we receive no information from the app.

Links in the app

The app's Contact Support button opens your email app with our support address, and the Privacy Policy button opens this page in your browser. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

Children

Levana is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
