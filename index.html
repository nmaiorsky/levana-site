Levana Support

Levana is a Hebrew calendar and moon app for iPhone: a ring of every day in the Hebrew month with its moon phase, Rosh Chodesh and holidays, Shabbat times for your city, and each month's symbols, wisdom, reflections and recipes.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: email Levana.app.contact@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Times look wrong for my city. Open Settings in the app and check Location. Choose "Change location" and search for your city, or tap "Use my location". Times are shown in that city's local time.

Holidays show one day instead of two (or the reverse). Settings › Calendar & Moon › Holiday calendar lets you choose Israel or outside Israel. It never changes on its own.

I'm not getting reminders. Turn them on in Settings › Notifications and allow notifications for Levana in your iPhone's Settings. Reminders are scheduled two months ahead and refreshed each time you open the app.

Where are my notes kept? On your iPhone only. See the Privacy Policy.
